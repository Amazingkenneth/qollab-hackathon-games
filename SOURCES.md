# Sources and scope

The story premises, dialogue, puzzles, scene layouts, art prompts, music recipes, supervisor workflow, and selection rubric in this pack are original design specifications. Technical sources below support the platform/API and scientific background. The independent NumPy calculations support the numerical examples. Source metadata was consulted during preparation on 10 October 2026; recheck live platform and license pages when building.

## Quantum models

- [IBM Qiskit quantum_info API](https://quantum.cloud.ibm.com/docs/en/api/qiskit/quantum_info): state/density/operator tools for the building agent's reference implementation.
- [IBM: discrimination and tomography](https://quantum.cloud.ibm.com/learning/en/courses/general-formulation-of-quantum-information/general-measurements/discrimination-and-tomography): background for nonorthogonal-state discrimination.
- [IBM: Naimark's theorem](https://quantum.cloud.ibm.com/learning/en/courses/general-formulation-of-quantum-information/general-measurements/naimark-theorem): implementing generalized measurements through a larger space.
- [IBM: limitations on quantum information](https://eu-de.quantum.cloud.ibm.com/learning/en/courses/basics-of-quantum-information/quantum-circuits/limitations-on-quantum-information): no-cloning background.
- [Elitzur and Vaidman, Quantum Mechanical Interaction-Free Measurements](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9305002): primary research background for Ghostlight Museum.

The bridge Hamiltonian, scene thresholds, teaching measurement families, copier strategies, and compact circuits are specified directly in the guides. They are small ideal models with stated assumptions. No computational speedup, universal counterfeiting optimum, real-device noise equivalence, or literal macroscopic story physics is claimed.
