# Asset credits and provenance

All artwork and audio are authored in local project code. The gallery, glass vitrine, paper orchid, conservator, apparatus, detector marks, and interface are inline SVG/CSS. The ambient loop and outcome cues are generated with Web Audio. No external images, fonts, recordings, or downloaded sound effects are included.

The asset-level records, source hashes, modification notes, and usage are in [`assets/manifest.json`](assets/manifest.json). These original procedural assets have no third-party license dependency. External art and audio were not acquired.

The original Elitzur–Vaidman paper is linked as optional reading in the game documentation; it is not bundled as an asset.
