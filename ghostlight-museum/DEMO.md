# Ninety-second route

1. From the foyer, choose **Enter the gallery**.
2. On the empty reference, leave the initial 90° mismatch visible, then choose **0°**. The exact empty-case Dark probability drops to zero. Optionally send the 64-shot calibration sample; its histogram is labelled as finite evidence.
3. Sign the empty reference. In the evidence desk, compare the empty and occupied prediction rows, then use **This trial**. Prepare one fresh replica, send one probe, and skip its animation if you want to keep the route moving.
4. Classify the live record from what was observed. Bright stays inconclusive; Dark confirms presence without absorption in that branch; Absorbed confirms presence with a cost.
5. Classify the three fixed **Reference reports** in order: Dark → presence without absorption, Bright → inconclusive, Absorbed → presence by absorption. These are explicitly labelled illustrative model cases.
6. Choose **π/6** in the protocol room. The evidence rail shows Dark 18.75%, Bright 56.25%, Absorbed 25%. Confirm to reach the signed report.

The live outcome is random and can be Bright, Dark, or Absorbed. The story advances through the correct interpretation in every case, so the demonstration does not depend on a lucky sample.
